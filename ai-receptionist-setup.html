Honest limits

What it does — and what it doesn’t.

An AI receptionist is a great employee with a clear job description. Here is exactly where that description ends, so there are no surprises after go-live.

It handles

  • Answering every call, 24/7, in English and Spanish (plus Russian)
  • Answering price and service questions from the prices you set
  • Booking jobs into your schedule and your job list
  • An instant alert to you for every booking, with name, job, and time
  • Turning anything else into a callback request — never a dropped call

It doesn’t

  • Take payments or card numbers over the phone
  • Negotiate prices or promise discounts you didn’t set
  • Handle complicated or sensitive calls — those become callback requests for you
  • Replace you: your customers can always reach a human — you — by callback

Not sure if your callers’ questions fit? That’s what the setup visit is for — we train it on the calls you actually get, and anything outside them routes to you.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the AI receptionist.

Does it sound robotic?

It speaks in a natural voice, at a natural pace — but don’t take our word for it. Call our own number, (661) 485-3595: the same system answers. Ask it for a price or book a job, and judge it the way your customers will.

Does it really speak Spanish?

Yes — English and Spanish, fluently, plus Russian. A caller who speaks Spanish gets the full conversation in Spanish: prices, questions, and the booking, not a translated voicemail.

What if a caller needs a human?

Then it takes a callback request: their name, their number, and what they need — and alerts you right away. The caller is never stuck arguing with a machine, and you never lose the lead.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes — your number stays yours. Most businesses simply forward their existing number to the receptionist: customers dial the same number they always have, and the receptionist answers. If you ever cancel, you stop forwarding and your number works exactly as before.

Can I change my prices later?

Anytime — and you don’t have to learn a dashboard to do it. Tell us the new price or the new service, and we update your receptionist for you. Updates are included in the monthly service.
