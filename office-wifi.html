Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about office Wi-Fi.

Why is one good router not enough?

Wi-Fi weakens through every wall, and an office has more walls, more people, and more devices than a home. One router in a corner gives you exactly what you have now: strong signal at three desks and drops everywhere else. Properly placed access points share one network and hand your devices off as you move.

Do we need to buy equipment first?

No. Tell us the space and we send an exact buy-list — router, switch, access points — with no markups. Or we work with what you already have if it is up to the job, and we will tell you honestly if it is not.

Can guests use Wi-Fi without seeing our computers?

Yes — that is the guest network setup. Visitors get internet on a separate network that cannot reach your workstations, shared files, or payment terminals. For shops and clinics that take cards, this separation matters.

Will this fix our dropped video calls?

If the cause is coverage or a struggling router — which it usually is — yes. If the cause is your internet plan itself, the coverage test will show it, and we will tell you exactly what to ask your provider for instead of selling you hardware.

What areas do you cover?

The Santa Clarita Valley — Santa Clarita, Valencia, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic. Include your ZIP in the request so availability is confirmed before scheduling.
