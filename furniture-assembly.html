Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about furniture assembly.

How much does assembly cost?

Standard items — beds, dressers, desks, bookcases, tables — start at $89 per item; wardrobes and large furniture start at $199. Several items in one visit are quoted together as one list. Your exact total is confirmed in writing before the visit, and it does not grow on the day.

How long does assembly take?

A typical item takes about 45–90 minutes; a wardrobe usually runs 2–3 hours. A whole room of furniture is normally finished in one visit. Your price is flat per item, so a stubborn bolt never changes the bill.

Do you bring your own tools?

Yes — a full tool kit, including the drivers flat-pack really needs and proper anti-tip hardware for anchoring tall pieces to wall studs. You do not need to supply anything, not even the famous Allen key.

What happens to the boxes and packaging?

We break down every box and stack the cardboard neatly by your recycling bin at no extra charge, and the room is left tidy. If you would rather keep a box for a possible return, just say so before we start.

How soon can you come out?

Appointments are usually available the same week, Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–6 PM, across the Santa Clarita Valley. Send the request with what you bought and your ZIP — we reply with the flat total and the earliest slots, in writing.

Which brands do you assemble — and what if parts are missing?

IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon, Target, Walmart — any flat-pack furniture with instructions. If a panel arrives damaged or hardware is missing, we stop instead of forcing it, show you exactly what is wrong, and document it so the retailer can send the replacement.
