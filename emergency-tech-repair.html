Urgent, never sloppy

Fast help that still follows the rules.

An emergency is exactly when corners get cut and passwords get handed over. Not here — these rules hold on every urgent visit too.

Always

  • You type your own passwords — we never ask for, write down, or store them
  • Backup status is checked before drive or cleanup work begins
  • The price is confirmed in writing before any work beyond the visit itself
  • Everything is tested with you — power, network, printing — before we call it fixed
  • You get a plain-language summary of what was wrong and what we did

Honest stop points

  • A drive that is clicking or failing: we stop — that is a data-recovery lab’s job, and we will say so
  • Liquid damage, broken screens, and board-level repair: referred to a repair shop
  • Employer- or school-managed devices: we do not remove their management or locks
  • Ransomware with encrypted files: we secure the machine and your accounts; nobody honest can promise the files back

Not sure if your problem counts as urgent? If work is stopped because of it, it counts. Send the form — we review every request and answer honestly.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about urgent tech help.

How fast can you actually get here?

During business hours (Mon–Sat, 8 AM–6 PM) we confirm urgent requests within the hour. Requests that come in by early afternoon get a same-day visit; later requests get the first slot the next business day. If same-day honestly isn’t possible, we tell you on the confirmation call — not after you’ve waited all day.

What does the $149 emergency callout cover?

It’s the fee for a guaranteed priority visit: we move you to the front of the day’s route and come out the same day. It covers the trip, the diagnosis, and the first fix work on site. If the repair needs more — a part or a bigger job — you get the full price in writing and approve it before anything else happens.

What if it can’t be fixed on the spot?

Then you get a clear plan and a full price before any further work: what’s wrong, what it takes, what it costs. Nothing proceeds on a vague “we’ll see.” And if the honest answer is a repair shop or a data-recovery lab, we say so — and what to tell them.

Do you only help offices?

No — homes too. A dead printer before a school deadline or a laptop that won’t start gets the same urgent treatment. Offices tend to feel the downtime harder, so several services on this page are written for them.

Which areas get same-day visits?

Santa Clarita, Valencia, Canyon Country, Newhall, Stevenson Ranch, Saugus, and Castaic. We’re based in the valley, so “same day” means a short drive across your neighborhood — not a dispatch promise from a call center in another state.
