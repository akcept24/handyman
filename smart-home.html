Set up right, from the start

Your accounts. Your home. Your rules.

Smart devices sit on your network and watch your front door. How they are set up matters as much as where they are screwed in.

Always

  • Every account is created in your name — you type every password, and nothing is registered to me
  • Two-step sign-in turned on for camera and lock apps, and sharing settings reviewed with you
  • Wi-Fi signal tested at the exact spot before a camera or doorbell goes up — if it won't reach, I say so before we drill anything
  • Alerts tuned with you: people and packages, not every passing car
  • You get a short walkthrough and a plain-language summary — how to view, share with family, and change codes

Honest stop points

  • New wiring, new circuits, or running cable through walls and attics: licensed electrical work — referred, not improvised
  • A thermostat that needs new HVAC wiring or a system change: that is an HVAC call, and I will tell you before touching it
  • Alarms that require a monitoring contract or a permit: explained honestly, and left to the companies that do them

Not a licensed contractor. Requests are limited to casual, minor work under $1,000 total, including labor and materials, where no permit, employees, or licensed trade is required. Larger projects may not be divided to fit the limit. Other work will be declined or referred.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about smart home setup.

Do I need to buy the devices before the visit?

Either way works. If you already bought them, I'll set up what you have. If you haven't, tell me what you want the home to do and I'll recommend the exact models first — it is common to overpay for the wrong doorbell or a camera that doesn't work with your other devices.

Will the app be on my phone, in my name?

Yes — always. Accounts are created in your name, you type every password, and I show you how to share access with family and how to remove it. Nothing is registered to me, and I can't see your cameras or codes after I leave.

Can you run wires through the walls or attic?

No — I set up battery and Wi-Fi devices and work with the wiring your home already has. New circuits, in-wall cable runs, and anything needing a permit are licensed electrical work, and I'll refer you rather than improvise it.

What if my Wi-Fi is weak where the camera needs to go?

I test the signal at the exact spot before anything is mounted. If it won't reach reliably, I'll say so — and the usual fix is coverage work first, like a mesh node. See the Wi-Fi & mesh page for how that visit works.

Is my camera video private?

The account is yours, protected by your password and the two-step sign-in we turn on together. During setup I show you exactly who can see what, how sharing works, and how to check which devices are signed in — so there are no surprises later.
