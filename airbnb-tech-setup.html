Set up right, from the start

Your listing. Your accounts. Your codes.

Guests, cleaners, and future hosts will all touch this tech after we leave. How it's set up decides who can get in — and who can't.

Always

  • Every account and the lock itself are set up in your name — you type every password and you hold the master code
  • Each booking gets its own guest code that expires at checkout; the cleaner's code is separate and revocable anytime
  • Guests join a guest Wi-Fi network where the router supports it, kept apart from your own devices
  • We run a mock guest check-in together before we leave — code, door, Wi-Fi, TV — exactly what your next guest will do
  • You get a printed host cheat sheet: codes, networks, batteries, and the two-minute fixes for the calls guests actually make

  • We don't keep copies of your codes — rotate the master code after setup if you'd like it to be yours alone

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about host tech setup.

Do I need to change locks between guests?

No — that's the point of doing this with codes instead of keys. The lock itself stays on the door; what changes is the code. Each booking gets its own code that starts working at check-in and stops at checkout, so a past guest's code never opens your door again.

What if the Wi-Fi dies in the middle of a stay?

Call us — hosts get priority, because a dark router is a bad review in progress. Most failures are a router that needs a reboot or a setting we can talk you through in minutes; if it needs a visit, we'll tell you honestly how fast we can be there. The cheat sheet also covers the two-minute fixes you or your cleaner can do on the spot.

Which locks work for this?

Most keypad smart locks from Schlage, Yale, August, Kwikset, and similar brands — or we supply a proven model as part of the visit. What matters is that it fits your door's existing preparation and supports multiple codes with schedules. Send a photo of your current door hardware with your request and we'll confirm the fit before the visit.

Can you set up access for my cleaner?

Yes — your cleaner gets their own separate code, on its own schedule if you'd like, and it can be revoked the day you change cleaners. It is never the guest code and never your master code. The turnover tech check can rotate it between stays as well.

Is this a property management service?

No — this is tech setup only. We don't message your guests, clean, price your nights, or manage your listing, and we take no share of your bookings. You keep your listing, your guests, and your revenue; we make the lock, the codes, and the Wi-Fi behave.
