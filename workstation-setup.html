Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about workstation setup.

What exactly is included in the $149?

One complete seat: computer unboxed and fully updated, user account created, email and office apps signed in, monitor positioned (or mounted, if you have the arm), keyboard, mouse and dock connected, office network and printer configured, cables tidied, and a test login plus test print before we leave.

We have not bought the computers yet. Can you help?

Yes — tell us what the person will do all day and we give you an exact buy-list (model, memory, storage, monitor, dock) with no markups and no affiliate games. When the boxes arrive, we do the setup.

How long does one seat take?

Typically about an hour per seat once updates behave, longer with a data transfer. Five-seat team setups are usually done in one visit. Your flat price does not change if an update runs long — that is the point of flat.

Can you set up a desk at an employee’s home?

Yes — hybrid teams are normal now. Home workstations in the Santa Clarita Valley are set up the same way; see also our home office setup page.

What areas do you cover?

The Santa Clarita Valley — Santa Clarita, Valencia, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic. Include your ZIP in the request so availability is confirmed before scheduling.
