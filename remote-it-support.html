How remote access works

You stay in control of your computer. Always.

Remote help only works on trust, so the rules are simple and strict:

Always

  • A one-time session code that you start and you can end at any moment
  • You see everything on your screen, in real time, while we work
  • Nothing stays installed after the session — no silent access later
  • Password changes happen with you, on the call, and are never kept by us
  • A plain-language summary of what was wrong and what changed

Never

  • We never ask you to install permanent remote-access software “just in case”
  • We never work on a machine without the owner or an authorized employee present
  • We never copy your files, photos, or customer data off the machine
  • Anyone who calls YOU claiming to be support and asks for access is a scam — hang up and call us at the number on this page

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about remote support.

Is remote support safe?

With the rules on this page, yes: you start the session with a one-time code, you watch everything on your screen, you can end it at any moment, and nothing stays installed afterward. The dangerous version is the opposite — a stranger calling you and asking for access. That is always a scam, including if they use our name; real support starts with your request to the number on this page.

What if you cannot fix it remotely?

Then we say so, explain what the evidence points to, and quote the on-site visit if you are in our service area. The $79 session fee is credited toward that visit, so you never pay twice for the same problem.

How fast can a session happen?

Requests are reviewed the same business day, Monday to Saturday, 8 AM–6 PM Pacific, and most sessions run the same day. Tech Care members go first in line.

What do I need on my side?

The computer, an internet connection, and about two minutes to open the session link we send. No software to install in advance, no accounts to create.

Can you help an employee who works from home?

Yes — that is one of the best uses of remote support. The employee connects the session from their home desk, you get one invoice and one summary, and nobody drives anywhere.
