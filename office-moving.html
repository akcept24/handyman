How a move should run

Nothing lost, nothing guessed, nothing “we’ll figure it out Monday.”

An office move touches every cable your business runs on. These are the rules every move follows — and the honest limits of what we do.

Always

  • Photo inventory of every workstation before anything is unplugged
  • Every cable and device labeled, so each desk goes back together exactly right
  • One fixed quote in writing after a free walkthrough — no hourly meter
  • Every workstation tested before we leave: power, network, and printing
  • One point of contact who coordinates the movers, the tech, and the schedule

Honest stop points

  • We are not a moving company: transport is provided by our licensed, insured moving partners
  • Rack-mounted servers and server rooms are quoted case by case, together with our partners
  • New in-wall cabling or electrical work that needs a permit or a licensed trade is referred to that trade
  • California Handymen is not a licensed contractor; our own hands-on work stays within minor-work scope under California law

Not sure whether your move fits? Tell us the number of workstations and your move date — we review every request and answer honestly, including when a piece of it belongs to a different specialist.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how an office move works.

How far ahead should we book?

Two to three weeks is ideal — it leaves time for the walkthrough, the fixed quote, and coordinating the moving crew for your date. Shorter timelines are often possible for small offices; tell us your date and we will answer honestly about what fits.

Do you move servers?

Workstation-level equipment — computers, monitors, docks, phones, and office printers — yes, that is the core of the service. Rack-mounted servers and full server rooms are quoted case by case together with our partners, because they need a shutdown plan and sometimes climate-controlled transport.

Can the move happen on a weekend or in the evening?

Yes — most office moves we coordinate run over a weekend or after hours, precisely so your team loses no workday. Friday-evening disconnect, weekend transport and setup, and testing before Monday 8 AM is the standard plan.

What areas do you cover?

The Santa Clarita Valley — Santa Clarita, Valencia, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic — and nearby areas case by case. If one office is in the valley and the other is just outside it, ask: include both ZIP codes in your request.

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes. After the free walkthrough you get one fixed number in writing for the scope we walked. It does not grow on move day. If you add workstations or equipment after the walkthrough, we quote the addition in writing before doing it — no surprises either way.
