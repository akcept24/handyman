Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

This policy explains how California Handyman ("we," "us," or "our") handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, requested service, project description, and any other information you choose to provide. Our hosting provider may also process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and security logs.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review your project, check service availability, respond to your request, arrange follow-up, maintain security, and comply with applicable law. Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment or create a service contract.

How information is shared

Estimate requests may be delivered through third-party communications and hosting providers acting on our behalf, including Telegram when configured for lead notifications. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users, our business, or others. We do not state that personal information submitted through this form is sold.

Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably needed for project follow-up, business records, security, and legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, but we use reasonable technical measures designed to protect submitted information.

Your choices and California privacy rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to identity verification and applicable exceptions. California residents may have additional rights under state law depending on the nature and scale of the business's data practices. To make a request, use the estimate form on the website and state that your message concerns privacy.

Children

This website is intended for adults requesting property services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest published version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, return to the estimate form and identify your message as a privacy request.
