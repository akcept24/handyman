How we work

Plain language. Tested before we leave. No mystery invoices.

Small offices do not need an enterprise IT contract. They need someone competent who answers, shows up, and fixes the thing. These are our rules — and our honest limits.

Always

  • Flat price in writing before work starts — no hourly meter
  • Everything tested with you before we leave: it prints, it connects, it starts
  • Plain-language explanation of what was wrong and what we changed
  • Your passwords and data stay yours — we never keep copies
  • One point of contact who knows your office and your setup

Honest stop points

  • Rack-mounted servers and server rooms are quoted case by case
  • New in-wall cabling or electrical work that needs a permit or a licensed trade is referred to that trade
  • We do not sell hardware with markups — we set up what you buy, or tell you exactly what to buy

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about small-office IT support.

How fast can you come?

Requests are reviewed the same business day, Monday to Saturday, 8 AM–6 PM. Urgent problems — a workstation or the office printer down — get priority, and anything that can be fixed remotely usually is, the same day. If your problem is a true emergency, see the emergency tech repair page.

What does Office Tech Care actually include?

A standing support relationship for your office, from $79/mo depending on office size: priority response when something breaks, remote fixes first, on-site visits at member rates, help with new-hire setups, and a team that already knows your computers, network, and printers. No long contract — cancel anytime.

Do you support servers?

Workstations, office printers, Wi-Fi, and everyday software — yes, that is the core. Rack-mounted servers and server rooms are quoted case by case, and we will tell you honestly when a problem belongs to a specialized provider.

Can you just fix it remotely?

Often, yes — software, email, printer queues, and account problems are regularly solved in one secure remote session for $79, and you watch everything on your screen. See the remote IT support page. Hardware problems need a visit, and we will say so up front.

What areas do you cover?

On-site: the Santa Clarita Valley — Santa Clarita, Valencia, Stevenson Ranch, and Castaic. Remote sessions: anywhere. Include your ZIP in the request so availability is confirmed before scheduling.
