# California Handymen — Furniture Assembly Marketing Kit

This folder contains owner-review assets for the Santa Clarita and Valencia furniture-assembly offering.

## Safety rules

- These files are **not wired to automatic sending**.
- A request/estimate email is transactional only when it relates to a lead that asked to be contacted.
- Any ongoing marketing, review request, referral prompt, or seasonal follow-up requires a **separate affirmative marketing opt-in** with timestamp/source retained by the sender.
- Do not send texts/emails to purchased lists, scraped contacts, or people who did not request contact.
- Do not claim licenses, insurance, 24/7 service, emergency service, same-day availability, guaranteed pricing, reviews, or availability that has not been confirmed.
- Never send a lead’s address, phone, email, or project notes to an unapproved third party.

## Contents

- `furniture-email-kit.html` — responsive preview of four client email templates.
- `notification-playbook.md` — owner notification and approved follow-up workflow.
- `offline-furniture-kit.html` — print stylesheet with flyer, business card, and vehicle/fridge magnet artwork.

## Before sending anything

1. Choose an approved sender platform (Google Workspace, Resend, or another owner-approved provider).
2. Configure a verified sending domain, reply-to inbox, and unsubscribe handling for marketing messages.
3. Keep transactional and marketing audiences separate.
4. Test to an owner-controlled inbox only.
5. Owner approves every live automation, audience, and copy change.

## Offline distribution guardrails

Obtain permission before leaving material at apartment communities, local businesses, community boards, or events. Follow each property’s posting rules and local solicitation rules. Do not place materials in mailboxes or on vehicles unless explicitly permitted.
