# Furniture Assembly — Offline Distribution Plan

## Goal

Put a clean, local, truthful furniture-assembly offer in places where people have just moved, bought furniture, or are planning a home setup project.

## Best first channels

1. **Apartment and condo communities — with property-manager permission**
   - Offer a small stack of flyers for a resident board, move-in packet, or lobby display.
   - Best message: furniture assembly for beds, desks, TV stands, bookcases, and dressers.
   - Ask the manager for the permitted placement and removal date.

2. **Local furniture / mattress / home-office stores — with owner permission**
   - Leave business cards at a counter only if the store agrees.
   - Do not present the business as affiliated with IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon, Costco, or any retailer unless that relationship exists in writing.

3. **Community boards**
   - Libraries, coffee shops, community centers, laundromats, gyms, and local independent businesses may have approved boards.
   - Photograph or log each permitted placement and check it once a month.

4. **Move-in / real-estate partner relationships**
   - Build a small referral card for realtors, movers, organizers, and cleaners only after they agree to share it.
   - Keep the pitch simple: “a local furniture assembly request option for clients settling in.”

5. **Vehicle / fridge magnet**
   - Give magnets to completed customers only if they want one.
   - It is a long-term reminder, not a claim of a formal partnership or guaranteed response.

## First 30-day field test

```text
Print: 100 flyers + 250 business cards + 50 magnets
Distribute only with permission
Start with 5–10 approved locations/partners
Use a distinct offline UTM link per format when tracking is ready
Review monthly: scans/visits, calls, submitted forms, qualified requests
Keep only placements producing qualified local requests
```

## Never do

- Do not place material in or on mailboxes without explicit authorization.
- Do not leave flyers on cars or doors where local/property rules prohibit it.
- Do not claim a retailer, property manager, HOA, realtor, or community “recommends” the service unless they explicitly approved that wording.
- Do not use fake reviews, fake before/after work, invented prices, or “same-day” language.
- Do not promote work outside the verified minor-work scope.

## Print handoff

- Flyer: US Letter, 8.5 × 11 in, full color, 100 lb text or similar.
- Business card: 3.5 × 2 in, 16 pt matte; print front/back.
- Magnet: 3.5 × 2 in flexible magnet; verify the printer’s bleed/safe-area requirements.
- Before ordering: replace `[PHONE]` in `offline-furniture-kit.html` with the owner-approved public number, print one proof, and check readability from arm’s length.
