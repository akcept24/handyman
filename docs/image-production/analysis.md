# Image production analysis

## Purpose
Create a coherent, trustworthy visual system for a California handyman estimate landing page. Images are representative service-category photography, not claims of completed client work.

## Art direction
- Photorealistic premium local-service editorial photography
- Warm natural California daylight, neutral modern residential interiors
- Consistent charcoal workwear with one subtle safety-orange accent; no logos or text
- Competent, calm, tidy workmanship; realistic tools and PPE
- No visible customer faces, fake badges, review graphics, license claims, before/after labels, watermarks, or unsafe work
- Avoid overprocessed HDR, extreme wide angle, plastic skin, malformed hands/tools, floating objects, duplicated fixtures

## Delivery
Hero: portrait-friendly 4:5 composition with right-side subject and clean negative space.
Projects: landscape 3:2 compositions that survive center cropping to cards.
